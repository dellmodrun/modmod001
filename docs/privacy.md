# Privacy

## Compliance

Formant is SOC 2 compliant and has a SOC 2 Type 2 report. The auditor is [Insight Assurance](https://insightassurance.com/).

The [Trust Center](https://trust.hacktron.ai) has the report and continuous, real-time evidence of Formant's active controls.

## AI providers

Formant uses these AI providers:

- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/)
- [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)
- [Google](https://ai.google.dev/)

None of them may train on Formant's data. Formant has signed **Zero Data Retention (ZDR)** agreements with all three, so data is not logged for human review, saved to disk, or retained on their servers.

More information is available on request. See also the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
