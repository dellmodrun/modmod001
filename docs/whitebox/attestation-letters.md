# Attestation letters

An attestation letter confirms which application Hacktron assessed and the period of the engagement. Share it with auditors or customers who need evidence of the pentest.

## Request a letter

After the pentest, email [hello@hacktron.ai](mailto:hello@hacktron.ai) to request an attestation letter. Include your organisation, the application, and the scan, so the team can issue it.

## View, download, or share a letter

Once the letter is issued, open **Attestation letters** in the dashboard sidebar. Each letter lists the customer, application, test type, engagement period, and issue date.

Open the letter's actions menu and choose:

- **Open** to view the letter
- **Copy link** to share it
- **Download PDF** to save a copy

:::warning
Anyone with the shared link can view and download the letter without signing in. Share it only with the people who need it.
:::
