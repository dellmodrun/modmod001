What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. We may also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs, and similar identifiers to recognize your browser or device, remember preferences, analyze usage, and support service functionality.

Third-party services

Legal basis

Managing your choices

You can also manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling some technologies may affect site functionality.

Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose. They may be:

  • Session cookies: Removed when you close your browser.
