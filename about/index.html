Findings

Formant gives you the offensive security capability to find and fix exploitable vulnerabilities before attackers do.

  • Two bugs chained into the ChatGPT and Codex accounts of OpenAI employees in under 72 hours, shown with a harmless pull request. The Washington Post

  • A pre-auth remote code execution bug (CVSS 9.9) in BeyondTrust Remote Support and PRA. Attackers were exploiting it within four days of disclosure. Google Threat Intelligence Group

  • A shell injection in GNU’s code forge led to root, and the access could have let an attacker push to Bash’s upstream repository. The FSF fixed 26 vulnerabilities. iTnews

  • Claude Opus built a working Chrome V8 exploit chain against the Chrome 138 bundled in Discord for about $2,283 in API fees. The Register

  • A sign-in bypass in Palo Alto’s GlobalProtect VPN that needs only a username. Palo Alto rated it High (7.2). Palo Alto Networks advisory

  • Five WAF bypasses in Vercel’s React2Shell challenge earned $170k. Vercel

Zayne Zhang

Chief Executive Officer

Zayne Zhang is Co-Founder and CEO of Formant. He has been credited with 15 CVEs, including three in the Node.js HTTP parser, and ranked first in the Singapore Ministry of Defence’s 2022 bug bounty and in three private Singapore government programs. He placed second at the DEF CON CTF finals in 2023 and 2024 with Blue Water, and founded Social Engineering Experts, Singapore’s top-ranked CTF team. Previously, Zayne did security research at TikTok and audits for Cure53. He began his career at the Singapore Armed Forces’ Cyber Defence School, where he graduated top of his cohort. He studied computer science at Cambridge with first-class results before leaving to start Formant.

Mohan Pedhapati

Chief Technical Officer

Mohan Pedhapati is Co-Founder and CTO of Formant. He has found remote code execution in Discord, VS Code and Element, and his ElectroVolt research, presented at Black Hat USA and DEF CON in 2022, compromised 20 widely used Electron apps, including Microsoft Teams. At Formant his team earned $170,000 of the more than $1 million Vercel paid for React2Shell firewall bypasses, and he found critical vulnerabilities in OpenAI’s ChatGPT Atlas and Perplexity’s Comet browsers. Previously, Mohan was a senior security auditor at Cure53 and founded Electrovolt, a security consultancy that grew to €1.5 million in revenue.

Harsh Jaiswal

Chief Research Officer

Harsh Jaiswal is Co-Founder and Chief Research Officer at Formant. His remote code execution findings on Meta’s and Apple’s servers earned bounties of $115,000 and $50,000. At Formant he and Mohan found CVE-2026-1731, a pre-authentication RCE in BeyondTrust Remote Support with a CVSS score of 9.9. He led the research that reached OpenAI’s internal code repository through employees’ ChatGPT and Codex accounts, and the audit that gained root on GNU Savannah, which hosts Bash. Previously, Harsh was a security researcher at ProjectDiscovery, where he published critical vulnerabilities in GitHub Enterprise, GitLab, Ivanti and Kubernetes Ingress-NGINX. Earlier, he was a security engineer at Vimeo and Zomato. He began hunting bugs on HackerOne in 2016.

Fabian Faessler

Head of Agent Engineering

Fabian Faessler is Head of Agent Engineering at Formant. He is LiveOverflow, whose YouTube channel teaches hacking to more than a million subscribers, and he co-founded Hextree with Thomas Roth. Google sponsors Hextree’s Android hacking courses, which the two built with Google’s Mobile Vulnerability Reward Program team. Previously, Fabian was a senior penetration tester at Cure53, where his audits from 2013 to 2023 included 1Password, Mullvad, SecureDrop and Ethereum’s Mist wallet. He holds a master’s in computer science from TU Berlin.
